1. **Kill shots.** For EACH other position, its 1–3 most dangerous concrete scenarios: a step-by-step timeline

5. **Concessions.** Which of your own round 1 choices you now think were wrong, including because of `facts.md`. An
   advocate concedes only to a fact, by number.
