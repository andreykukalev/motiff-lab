# MIDI test fixtures

Small Standard MIDI Files for the importer, hand inference, rhythm restoration and the import performance target. Every file is generated by [`make_fixtures.py`](make_fixtures.py); none contains published music. Run `python3 make_fixtures.py` in this folder to rebuild them; the output is byte-for-byte deterministic (the "played" timing comes from a fixed generator in the script, not from Python's `random`). `MidiFixtureTests` pins what each file must import as.

| File | Format, PPQ | What it is | Exercises |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `scale-type0.mid` | 0, 96 | C major scale up and down in quarters, 100 BPM, 4/4; running status and velocity-zero note-offs | Type 0 reading, PPQ that divides 480 |
| `two-hands-type1.mid` | 1, 480 | Four-bar waltz: conductor, right hand, left hand (sustain pedal, program change) and a channel 10 click | Tracks, tempo change, pedal dropped, drums left out |
| `performance-type1.mid` | 1, 1000 | A short played phrase with no tempo or meter: a re-struck key, a stray note-off, a zero-length note and a hanging note | Rounding (PPQ 1000 does not divide 480), every repair policy in 0005 |
| `piano-single-track.mid` | 0, 480 | Eight bars of piano, both hands on one track and channel, quantised, 84 BPM, 4/4. In bars 5 and 6 the hands share the E4 region, so a fixed split point fails | S1-I4, AC-S1-04: hand inference. [`piano-single-track.hands.txt`](piano-single-track.hands.txt) gives the intended hand of all 55 notes |
| `performance-click.mid` | 1, 480 | Eight bars played to a 96 BPM click (no click track in the file): 16th and 8th figures plus a human offset of up to ±40 ticks, legato lengths, varied velocities; 128 notes on two tracks | S1-I6, S1-R1, AC-S1-05: the tempo map fits, so Restore rhythm is offered. About 42% of onsets are more than 10 ticks off the 16th grid, with a mean offset of 0.09 of a 16th |
| `free-tempo.mid` | 0, 480 | A rubato melody with bass, 46 notes, recorded without a click. It declares 120 BPM and 4/4 (sequencer defaults), but the beat drifts between 0.45 s and 1.3 s | S1-R2, AC-S1-06: onsets are spread evenly against every common grid (mean offset about a quarter of the grid step), so it cannot be restored in Stage 1 |
| `drums-channel10.mid` | 0, 480 | Two bars at 110 BPM: 12 piano notes on channel 1 and 24 drum hits on channel 10, interleaved on one track | S1-I2: drums left out with a notice, piano kept |
| `no-time-signature.mid` | 1, 240 | A 24-note waltz tune at 76 BPM with a key signature but no time signature | S1-I3: 4/4 assumed with a notice (six bars of 4/4) |
| `large-5000-notes.mid` | 1, 480 | Exactly 5,000 quantised notes over 200 bars at 120 BPM: 16th arpeggios (3,200) and left-hand 8ths with a bass note (1,800) | S1-I1, AC-S1-01: import performance target |

The files stay small (the largest, `large-5000-notes.mid`, is about 41 KB), so they live in the repository.
