# MusicXML test fixtures

MusicXML 4.0 files for the MusicXML importer. Every file is generated by [`make_fixtures.py`](make_fixtures.py); none contains published music. Run `python3 make_fixtures.py` in this folder to rebuild them; the output is byte-for-byte deterministic (the `.mxl` archive has fixed timestamps). All four `.musicxml` files validate against the MusicXML 4.0 XSD. `MusicXmlFixtureTests` pins their structure; the importer's own tests should pin what each must import as.

Like most files exported by notation apps, each `.musicxml` starts with a `<!DOCTYPE>` that points to the public DTD on musicxml.org. An importer must not fetch it (nothing leaves the device in Stage 1): read with DTD processing ignored.

| File | What it is | Exercises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ties-triplets-pickup.musicxml` | One part, 4/4, G major, 88 BPM, divisions 6. A one-beat pickup (bar 0, `implicit="yes"`), a tie inside bar 1, a tie across the bar line from bar 3 to bar 4, triplet 8ths (bars 1 and 4) and triplet quarters (bar 2). The last bar has three beats, completing the pickup. 20 written notes, 18 after merging ties | AC-S1-02, S1-I2, S1-I3: tied notes become one note (D5 of 9 divisions in bar 1, G5 of 18 divisions over bars 3–4), triplet onsets are exact at 480 ticks per quarter, and bar 1 starts after the pickup at tick 480 |
| `septuplets.musicxml` | One part, 4/4, C major, divisions 7. Septuplet 16ths (7:4, one division each) in bars 1 and 2, septuplet 8ths (7:4 over a half, two divisions each) in bar 1. 25 notes | AC-S1-03, 0002: 21 notes (every septuplet note) have an onset or end that cannot be a whole tick and must be reported as rounded; the 4 plain notes are exact. No boundary may move by more than half a tick |
| `grand-staff.musicxml` | One part, "Piano", two staves (treble and bass), 3/4, F major, 96 BPM, divisions 2. Chords (`<chord/>`), `<backup>` from staff 1 to staff 2, a rest on staff 2, a second voice on staff 1 in bar 4, a B flat. 26 notes: 11 on staff 1 (voices 1 and 2), 15 on staff 2 (voice 5) | S1-I4: hands split by staff and marked imported; voices and track names kept |
| `grand-staff.mxl` | `grand-staff.musicxml` compressed: a stored `mimetype` entry first, `META-INF/container.xml` naming `score.musicxml` as the root file, and the score | S1-I1: compressed MusicXML |
| `phrases-repeats.musicxml` | One part, 4/4, C major, 100 BPM, divisions 4. A forward repeat at bar 1, a backward repeat at the end of bar 4, first and second endings (bars 4 and 5). Phrase slurs (`number="1"`) over bars 1–2, 3–4 and 5; a nested slur (`number="2"`) from E5 to F5 in bar 1; a quarter rest at the start of bar 3. 15 notes, one rest | S1-I5: phrase boundaries only where the source supports them (slurs, a beat of rest, repeat signs) |
