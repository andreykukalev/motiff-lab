# UI conventions

Rules for the web UI (`src/MotiffLab.Web`) as it is on main after the redesign (decision record 0018), so a fix in one corner reads like the rest. The native iPad app (0019) follows these rules in SwiftUI where it has the same function; which functions it has, and in which form, follows 0025 (two workspaces, no parity). Where this file and `design/kit` disagree, the kit's documents win (`design/kit/HANDOFF.md` says which source wins), and this file is corrected in the same PR.

## What the UI may do

- The UI only displays what music-core returns. It never changes analysis or variation code, or the shapes of their results, and it never works out a musical fact itself: no grouping, no key, no chord, no place in the score, no boundary. A short line for a fact is music-core's too (`MotifFact.Brief`, shown through `MotifFact.Claim`).
- Never build an experiment, a variation step, an observation or a session in the UI. Ask music-core (`ExperimentCatalogue`, `VariationChain`, `Project.Keep`, `NoteEditLog`) and draw what comes back.
- Anything inferred is marked inferred, by more than colour (a dashed line, a tag, words). Every fact keeps its certainty and its Evidence.
- Everything works with AI off. What the assistant adds is Pro; on the device is free; nothing names a server to a player except the privacy lines that say where a file goes.

## Design system

- Every colour, size, radius, shadow, font and timing is a token from `design/kit/tokens.css` (the web app serves a copy in `wwwroot/css`); a canvas reads them with `Scripts/layout/tokens.ts`. Components follow `design/kit/ds.css`. Do not port the mockup HTML (`s*.html`); port rules, tokens, icons and the Verovio options.
- One accent: cobalt (`--accent`) is pointing and selection only; plum is what the app composed and Pro; amber is what the player marked. The player's inks (carmine, graphite, ochre) do not change with the theme.
- Icons come from `wwwroot/img/icons.svg`, drawn in Razor with `<Icon Name="…" />`; `node tools/icons/build.mjs` writes the set for both apps.
- Switches use the kit's switch (`input[type=checkbox].switch` with `role="switch"`); overlines are uppercase; the piece's name is the serif title.
- A control a finger uses is at least 44px (`--h-touch`); a compact control is `--h-compact`. Text meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; the axe checks in `tests/e2e` run on every screen they reach.

## The score page

- The score is the whole screen (`Pages/PlaybackLab.razor`, `Score/ScoreView.razor`): one continuously scrolling document of A4 pages (0017; Verovio options in `Scripts/score/score-page.ts`, the same on the iPad) or, chosen in Settings · Score, a strip. Nothing is docked; there are no page jumps.
- The bar floats at the top: back to the library, the piece's title (it opens the Contents), the page count, and the actions Phrases, Pen, Piano roll, Insights, Play and More. It fades while the player reads and comes back on a tap (`Scripts/layout/bar-fade.ts`).
- More holds only what changes the whole score: which score shows (Reading | As played, with About this notation), a second file and what is heard from it, downloads. The layout (pages or strip) is in Settings · Score.
- The pill (the transport) appears with a selection: play, loop, tempo (the dial icon) and hands, each popover anchored to its button. The tempo (v0.5.4, #511 section 17, `Playback/TempoControl.razor`, on the iPad `Tempo.swift`) reads «90 % · ♩ 83» (♩ ≈ from a recording, ♩ ? when not recognised, ♩. in 6/8); its popover stays open while playing and closes with ✕ or a tap on the notes: the percentage and the beats a minute, each typed by a tap, − slider + by 1 % held to repeat (`Scripts/layout/hold-repeat.ts`), steps 50–110 %, «Темп пьесы» with its source, and «Темп в нотах…», which sets the piece's tempo and keeps the percentage. A long press on the pill, or on an action of the bar, opens that element's own settings (`Scripts/layout/long-press.ts`); only a held press swallows the click its release makes.
- Under the playback model (v0.5.2, #495 UX-10, the experiment Two playheads and modes, outside focus) the pill always shows and holds the one Play: cobalt where the selection plays, or stopped will play, ink where the piece goes on, named by the place as bar.beat («С 10.3», «Пауза · 10.3»); with a selection, Цикл with its span («Цикл 9.1 – 12.3»), on by itself with a new selection, taking the position to the selection's start when turned on. The bar and the selection's menu have no Play and no Loop then. The script's player owns the model (`Scripts/playback/playback-owner.ts`, the rules in `playback-model.ts` shared with the iPad); the page sends it events (`Pages/PlaybackLab.Playheads.cs`).
- Drawers and sheets rise over the score: the piano roll from the bottom (resized by its handle, `Scripts/layout/drawer-resize.ts`), Insights, the Contents, the Check sheet and a version's sheet. An element over the score that the selection menu must keep clear of carries `data-menu-clear`.
- A scan the server reported on page by page (version 0.4, `scan.pages`) shows a summary by page in the Check sheet and in the report after it is read: only the pages with bars in question, a faint or poor print, or no bars read («Страница 4: 9 из 12 тактов под вопросом», music-core's `ScanCheck.Pages`; `Import/ScanPagesSummary.razor`). Each bar of a poor page carries the Check flag «Плохая страница». Reports that do not match the pages of the notes are dropped and the notes kept.
- The piano roll (`Scripts/roll/piano-roll.ts`, testing items 11–13): rows of 22 px at 100 %, a note's name without its octave inside it where it fits, the octave named once in a band down the left, the hand told by colour and pattern alone. One zoom for the time and the rows floats over the corner of the notes (− the percentage + and Fit, no panel); a pinch and Ctrl and the wheel zoom too. Fit lays the selection, or the phrase in view, across the roll. Expand on its head takes the phrase into Focus as a roll; Focus switches Notes | Piano roll in its bar. Zoom and view are two states (v0.5.4, #511 sections 5–6, `Scripts/roll/roll-view.ts`): the player's zoom changes only by the zoom controls, a pinch, the wheel and Fit, which glides there in `--t-slow` and is then just the zoom; a selection, playback, Цикл, the notes coming back, a new size (the drawer's handle, a turn of the iPad) never change it (Expand lays its passage across and gives the zoom back on the way out), and the counts of bars and octaves follow from it and the size. The view moves by the player's scrolling, by Fit, and with playback: past 70 % of the width it goes along with the playhead, never by pages, and a playhead that jumped out of view is eased back once; a new selection wholly out of view is brought into it. The web's roll opened anew (the drawer again) starts from the zoom and view the player left. Expand is a focused practice view bound to the exact selection it was opened on (#521 19; `ipad/App/MotiffLab/Expand.swift`, on the web `Pages/PlaybackLab.Focus.cs`, the roll's `setPassage` and its `Passage` parameter): only with a selection (without one the roll's Expand is dimmed and says why), it shows only that passage, laid across the card with no scrolling when it fits and scrolling only within it when it does not (the roll's least zoom is the passage's, playback is followed no further than its end); nothing in it creates, changes or clears the selection or a loop, edits notes or steps to another phrase, and the stylus menu has no selection controls there. Play in it loops the passage, from the playhead inside it or its start, with no Loop to turn off and no Lead-in (#533 2, 3); its roll draws no selection band, and its playhead is taken by its handle (a finger anywhere down its line on the iPad) and dragged inside the passage, heard, playback standing while it is held and going on from where it is let go; holding the roll elsewhere still pauses following (#521 16). Playing, the roll's playheads are found where they were drawn in the last `--t-slow`, at most a touch target behind, so a finger that lands where it saw a moving line takes it (#533 4). The way back is named by the way in (#521 17): «К странице» from the score, «Свернуть» with the `collapse` icon from the roll, and it brings back the drawers, the roll's zoom and view, the selection and the loop as they were. The focus bar has no Play: the pill's is the one, and it always shows in Focus. The pill's Play is labelled with the place alone, bar.beat, at a fixed width. Expand (in the selection menu and the roll's head) has its own icon, `expand`, never Insights' magnifier.
- On the iPad the roll is where notes are edited (v0.5.2, #479 item 23; `ipad/App/MotiffLab/RollTools.swift`, `RollMode` and `RollInput` in `InputRules.swift`): Edit notes is not in the bar's modes but in the roll's head, «Навигация | Правка нот», the chosen one with its word and the other with its icon, in the drawer and in the expanded roll. In Navigate the Pencil and a finger move the roll every way; in Edit notes the Pencil edits and a finger moves the roll, and without a stylus one finger edits and two move it (a finger's Add or Remove acts when lifted). Navigate closes an editor with nothing changed and keeps a draft with changes, whose Cancel and Save stay in the head. The selection is the score's own: a drag across the bar numbers or a handle of the loop strip selects by beats where the score does, and the score, the pill and the loop strip follow. The zoom is per axis and in words (`Scripts/roll/roll-zoom.ts`, opted into by `setAxisZoom`; the web keeps its floating zoom): «− 9 тактов +» and Fit along the bottom right, «+ 3 октавы −» in a column over the keyboard, a pinch zooming the axis it spreads along while two fingers move the roll. The pill stays over the drawer, so Play is always reachable. The capsule at the top of the drawer is its handle (#511 section 7.1, `RollDrawerHeight` in `RollTools.swift`, the web's `drawer-resize.ts` rules): dragged from a fifth to 85 % of the screen, dragged nearly away it closes, a tap puts it away; the height is kept on the device for each orientation.
- The roll's playhead is the player's to move (`Scripts/roll/roll-playhead.ts`, v0.2). Its handle hangs under the bar numbers, so the bar numbers keep selecting bars: dragging the handle (on the iPad, a finger anywhere down the line, a touch target wide) moves the playhead, and the music plays on from there while it plays; paused, Play goes on from where it is. It stays inside the loop, else the selection, and the loop and the tempo stay. On the iPad a tap inside the loop or the selection plays from there, looping the selection when nothing loops yet; on the web a click there still picks notes. Its control is a range input over the handle: the arrow keys and VoiceOver's swipes move it by a beat (and ← → on the iPad's roll), and it says the bar and beat.
- Overlays on the score (brackets, bands, rings, bookmarks, pencil marks, check flags) are placed by `Scripts/score/score-geometry.ts` from the rendered `g.measure` and `g.staff` boxes, on pages and on the strip alike, and stored per bar, never per pixel.

## Phrases and the selection

- The score is the selection surface; there is no phrase list or strip. Phrases are brackets above the staves in three levels (short phrases, phrases, sentences; the level in the Phrases popover, for this piece or everywhere). An inferred bracket is dashed. A bracket across a system break draws a run on each system.
- A tap on a bracket selects the phrase; a second tap lifts it into Focus. A tap on a bar, or a drag on the piano roll, selects bars; Shift extends. A tap on the paper where there is no music puts the selection away. Escape does too.
- Page Up and Page Down step to the previous and next phrase anywhere on the page, except in a field and in Expand, which stays on its passage (#521 19) (`Scripts/phrases/phrase-keys.ts`); page-turner pedals send these keys. Space plays and pauses.
- The selection menu (`Scripts/score/selection-layer.ts`) floats over the selection: Roll, Bookmark, Note, Highlight, Focus, and More for the selection (Ask first, then making and merging phrases). Clearing the selection has its own icon, `deselect` (the selection's frame with a cross in it), wherever the action is: the selection menu on both apps, the roll's head and the stylus menu on the iPad (#533 section 5); the bare `close` cross only closes. Under 700px it shows icons only. On the iPad (`SelectionMenu` in `ipad/App/MotiffLab/ScoreScreen.swift`, v0.5.2, #495 UX-11) its actions are always icons alone, each a 44 pt square on the accent's tint while on, its word kept for VoiceOver; the web keeps its words (0025: Andrei asked about the iPad). It sits under the drawers and sheets and moves aside for any element marked `data-menu-clear`.
- The headings say where the player is in one line: "Phrase 1 · bars 0–4", "Nothing selected". What was restored when a piece opens is said once, not kept in a heading.

## Insights

- One stream (`Insights/InsightsStream.razor`), no Understand / Explore / Build tabs. It reads the workspace state (`Workspace/WorkspaceState.cs`), which the page builds for each render and cascades. Put state the stream needs into the snapshot, never into a new parameter or into the component.
- From the top: the lens chips (All and one per lens, one row that scrolls sideways), the ideas kept for the passage as plum chips with Manage, the one thing to hear first (`FactRanking.KeyObservation`), then the facts by lens.
- A fact is a short statement that points at the notes: its claim, its certainty, how many places, Evidence (the full listing when there is a brief, then the basis), and one quiet Try button. The stream leads with Main (`FactRanking.Main`, testing item 14): the harmonic motion and what it gives (`harmony.motion`), then the texture (`character.texture`), then the strongest fact of another lens, three at most. Every other fact sits under Details, folded by lens with its count (harmony and character first, then structure, line, rhythm and key), a tap opening a lens in `FactRanking.InStream` order; filtering to a lens opens it; the lens holding the chosen fact or the trial opens by itself.
- A claim with places is a toggle: it rings its notes on the score and lists its places as chips; a chip rings only its place and moves playback to it.
- Try opens the two or three ways music-core offers for the fact; a way sounds at once. Its card (`Explore/ExploreCard.razor`) leads with the sound: the title, the passage, the ways, what changed, Try another and Leave it. The idea, its question, what it keeps, what to listen for and the facts it rests on are under About this idea. Keep and Adjust follow the Try button on the row that holds the trial.
- The assistant's input closes the stream; it asks about the selection or the whole piece.

## Compare and ideas

- While a variation exists the inspector head has the compare switch (`Compare/CompareSwitch.razor`): Original | Variation and One after the other. A running loop keeps looping with the new choice; the engine switches on the exact frame (0007) and the score follows the one sounding.
- Compare (`Compare/ComparePanel.razor`) says what changed (from `Variation.Changes`) and what was kept (from the Keep checks); nothing is computed again in the UI.
- Keep this variation keeps it as an idea (`Observation`), with a note in the player's own words that nothing reads, scores or sends. Ideas are listed, opened, renamed, exported and deleted through `Project`; Delete asks first. Für Elise has no project, so what is kept lasts while the page is open, and the card says so.

## Marks

- The pen writes on the score (`Scripts/score/pencil-layer.ts`): a pen or the mouse with the pen on, never a finger, which scrolls. Marks are kept against their bar and are the player's writing, never music: no analysis reads them.
- On the iPad the stylus follows the tool (since 2026-11-13, #479 item 18, `ipad/App/MotiffLab/InputRules.swift`), in Notes and on a PDF's pages alike: in Navigate it moves the music, in Select it selects by beats, with the Pen it writes; a finger always scrolls, and a long press of a finger selects in any tool. Without a stylus (the iPad's «Only draw with Apple Pencil» off) one finger takes the tool and two scroll. A touch on a selection handle moves the handle and never writes. The stroke being written is the outline the page keeps (`outlinePath`, in Swift `InkOutline`), with its pressure and the pencil's grain (`ipad/App/Web/grain.png`), and the eraser cuts strokes along its path (`eraseAlong`).
- The pen palette (v0.5.6, #533 section 9; on the web `Score/PenPalette.razor`, on the iPad `PenState.swift`, `PenTools.swift`, `PenPalette.swift`, `PencilPalette.swift`), in Notes and on a PDF's pages: one card in two rows. The first stays put whatever the tool: exactly one tool, Pencil, Marker or Eraser, each its own kit icon (`pencil`, `marker`, `eraser`, never the bar's `pen` or the selection's `highlight`) in a `.seg` row; Undo and Redo of the pen's own history (the web adds Clear); the layers (Notes only) and the cross. Under a hairline the second holds the chosen tool's settings, grouped: its colour (the pencil's four inks as swatches, the chosen one ringed in the accent; the marker's one amber) and its thickness (lines in the tool's colour, as thick as it draws in proportion, the chosen one ringed); for the eraser one line on what it does. The card keeps the room of the pencil's row, so nothing moves under the finger. A setting changes only its tool and never switches tools, and each tool keeps its own settings. The palette the Pencil's double tap or squeeze opens has the same icons and samples: the tools on the inner half circle, the active tool's settings on the outer one. Every highlight is read from one state (`PenState`), so two things never look chosen at once.
- A bookmark is a bracket in its category's ink above the phrase brackets, its title in italic serif; across a system break each run keeps its hooks and repeats the title after "…", and a long title ends in an ellipsis. The Contents lists bookmarks by category, the detected form and the pages.
- Pieces review (the experiment Several pieces in a score or recording, Settings · Experimental, v0.5; it replaced Automatic bookmarks): while a piece keeps no pieces, the Contents open «Пьесы» with the pieces music-core proposes (`SectionFinder.FindPieces`; in a recording a pause alone is not a boundary), one row a piece: its number, its title to type, its bars and its time in a recording or its pages, and for a boundary found, dashed and tagged "found automatically", how sure it is and the facts it rests on, never in amber or a category's ink. A boundary is removed, moved by a bar or added from a bar typed or from the start of the selection (tagged "your boundary"); «Найти заново» asks music-core again, with the number of pieces when given (a recording added with «В записи несколько пьес» brings it). Nothing is saved until Confirm, which keeps them in one save as the pieces layer: ordinary bookmarks in the category Pieces (`PieceLayer`), listed there afterwards, a tap selecting each, until «Проверить пьесы» reviews them again (`Workspace/PiecesReview.razor`).
- Pieces in a file (part of the experiment Several pieces in a score or recording, Settings · Experimental; v0.4): the Contents list the pieces of a collection, its pieces layer (version 0.5), and «Разделить на пьесы» opens the list with each piece's bars (and, for a recording, its stretch of the sound, which music-core gives), a title, a composer and tags each and the playlist's name. Split asks music-core (`PieceParts.Split`; a recording's pieces on bars found from their own stretch) and saves each piece it makes as a new library piece in that playlist; the collection is not changed and then says how many pieces were made, and each piece says where it came from. Where each piece came from is read from its own file (version 0.4): the collection's original it keeps, named by the collection's title, and its place there (`partOf`: pages, or a recording's stretch; none for a score cut by bars). The collection finds its pieces among the library's rows (`ProjectSummary.Part`, `PieceParts.IsPartOf`), and a piece's card in the library says «Из «…»» with its place (`Workspace/PiecePartsSection.razor`). A piece split from a recording (version 0.5) keeps its own stretch of the sound only on the device that split it (cut from the stretch heard, which a recording added with «В записи несколько пьес» keeps there), described in its originals as `src_sound` and never in its file; it opens with Add a source, and its Contents switch between the recording's sound and the notes («Звук этой пьесы», `Workspace/PieceSound.razor`), or, on another device, ask for the recording's file again and check its stretch by that description. On the iPad, for a PDF or photo piece, the PDF's Contents open «Пьесы в этом файле»: a row per piece (title, first and last page with − and + at a fingertip's size), first filled from the page bookmarks, «Добавить пьесу» from the page under the bar, and the playlist; Split copies each piece's pages with PDFKit and makes it a PDF piece of its own (a photo piece's parts stay photos) whose project file says where it is in the collection (`partOf`); the library tells from the pieces' `partOf` and the originals' fingerprints what was split from what, kept nowhere else (`PiecePartsSheet.swift`, `PieceParts.swift`).
- Check by pieces (part of the experiment Several pieces in a score or recording, Settings · Experimental; version 0.5): when the piece keeps a pieces layer, the head of the Check drawer lists the pieces, each with its bars to check as music-core counts them (`CheckPieces`), Open (the list below keeps to that piece's bars; again for every piece) and Looks right, which resolves every flagged bar of the piece in one save, a scan confirming them as one bar's Looks right does; Undo beside it opens those bars again, the bars kept in memory only (`Workspace/CheckPieces.razor`). On the iPad (`Check.swift`), the same rows from `check`'s `pieces`, with Open and Looks right, without Undo; with the experiment on, «Пьесы в этом файле» of a PDF whose notes were read into the same piece adds «Предложить» (the rows from the pieces layer kept, else from music-core's proposals, by their pages) and «Подтвердить пьесы» (`pieces.save`, the layer synced with the web, then the PDF view opens the file again).
- My corrections (an experiment, Settings · Experimental, version 0.5): a piece opened from the device lays its corrections over the notes one by one (music-core's `NoteEditLog.ApplyEach`): one whose note changed a little is moved to the nearest note of the same pitch, one that no longer fits is listed, and a motif, variation or idea that no longer fits is hidden, never lost. Where the corrections are made (Insights · More tools · Correct notes, and the head of the Check sheet) a switch «Мои правки» says how many there are and how many did not fit, and lists those with their bar, note and reason and «Убрать», which takes one out of the edit log. Off, the notes are as recognised and cannot be corrected (the roll's editor goes), saving leaves the motifs and variations as they were, and the piece opens again so; whether it is off is kept on this device by the piece's id (`Projects/Preferences.cs`), never in the project (`Workspace/EditsLayerSwitch.razor`, `Pages/PlaybackLab.EditsLayer.cs`).
- Live performance tempo (an experiment, Settings · Experimental, version 0.5): a recording heard is given bars found afresh after each pause of 2.5 s and each jump in tempo (music-core's `BarGrid.FindLive`). Insights opens with a Tempo card for the piece (`Insights/TempoCard.razor`): music-core's `TempoProfile`, a curve bar by bar in ink over its usual range as a band, the usual tempo as a dashed line, the stretches that go slower or faster in teal and as chips that select their bars, and a line for each piece of the pieces layer; it is marked worked out for a recording. In the Check sheet's head for a recording, «Найти такты по разделам» and «Такты по партитуре» find the bars again with the pieces layer moving along (`LiveBars`), the second with a score version's metre and tempo as a hint; the status says how many sections and whether the score's metre fitted (`Workspace/LiveBarsActions.razor`, `Pages/PlaybackLab.Tempo.cs`). A piece split from a recording («Разделить на пьесы») finds its beat the same way in its own stretch (`PieceSplitOptions.LiveTempo`).
- For reading with the marks that matter (an experiment, Settings · Experimental, version 0.5): a recording's score «Для чтения», on the page and in focus, also carries the dynamics, rit., accel. and fermatas music-core picks (`PlayedMarks.Significant`): a dynamic only where the playing moves two steps or more and holds, rit. and accel. where the bars grow a fifth longer or shorter, a fermata over a chord held through a beat twice as long as the eight around it. Accents, hairpins, slurs and pedal stay in «Как сыграно». The score's note says the marks are worked out from the recording (`Pages/PlaybackLab.Reading.cs`); switching the experiment engraves the score again.
- Changes of signature in a recording (an experiment, Settings · Experimental, version 0.5, a preview): in the Check drawer's head for a transcription with bars, one line for each stretch music-core finds reading better in 5/8, 7/8, 9/8, 5/4 or 12/8 («Похоже на 7/8 в тактах 17–35», `LiveBars.OddMeters`), or that none does, and a note that it is a preview from where the bass falls (`Workspace/OddMetersPreview.razor`). Nothing is changed or saved.
- The Reference (an experiment, Settings · Experimental): Circle, a word in the library's head and in the score's bar, opens the circle of fifths as a sheet (`Reference/ReferenceSheet.razor`; on the iPad `ReferenceSheet.swift`). The key signatures come first; one marks the major and its relative minor and opens the major's card (`KeyReference`): the signature on a treble staff and in words, the relative, parallel and enharmonic keys, the scales and the three main chords spelled by letter, each played on the app's piano and shown on a small keyboard. In a piece the key it starts in is outlined, dashed when assumed (on the iPad too, from `reference.key` on the piece). The circle never opens empty (#533 7): on the piece's key where it is known, else on C major, only for display; over a piece with no known key (a PDF without notes, say) a caption under the circle says its key is not known. C major is not written anywhere and is never shown as the piece's key. A tap on a scale or main chord of a key card also opens its own card under it. Keys | Chords | Scales at the top of the sheet: Chords takes a root (the seventeen spellings) and a chord, and shows its symbol, degrees over notes, and root position and each inversion, each played together or broken (`ChordReference`); Scales takes a root and a scale (major, the three minors, the church modes, the two pentatonics) and plays it up and down (`ScaleReference`). Nothing is saved.
- A stroke begun in a line's margin says which (`margin`, version 0.4) and is drawn from that edge of whatever line its bar is on; a stroke without one is placed by `u` below 0 or above 1, as before (`Scripts/score/pencil-marks.ts`, shared with the iPad's score view).
- Layers (under Pen) show or hide the player's marks by kind.

## The library and the screens around it

- The library (`Pages/Home.razor`) shows the pieces as cards, each resumed where it was left; Add a piece opens one sheet with the routes in (Open a file, Scan a page, From a recording, the demo). The Pro routes say what to do in the player's words ("Sign in or get Pro").
- A screen a link leads to outside the score (opening a file, scanning a page, notes from a recording, the account, a page not found) is a sheet over the library (`Design/PageSheet.razor`): its title, a close back to the library, the paper dimmed behind.
- Settings is a sheet (`Settings/SettingsSheet.razor`) with its sections as tabs: Guidance, Score, Layers, Piano roll, Appearance, Account (with the scan server's address only in a build without Motiff Lab's own) and Experimental, which holds one switch per experiment (governance, "Experiments"), each off by default and kept on the device. A popover's settings name where they apply: this piece or everywhere.
- A link to a place on another screen (`settings#account`) lands there: the layout scrolls to the fragment.

## Localization

- Interface strings live in `wwwroot/i18n/en.json` and `ru.json`, read through `UiText` (`Text["key"]`, `Text.Format`, `Text.Count` with `.one`, `.few`, `.many`). Add every new string to both files; `tests/web/i18n.test.mjs` checks that every key used exists in both.
- Musical text (facts, briefs, bases, place labels) comes from music-core in the language the page passes (`Words.For(Text.Language)`); the UI never translates or rewrites it. Terms shared across screens live under `term.*` (0013 section 7).

## General

- Say it in one line: an empty state is one line and one action; a heading is one line; explanations open on request (Evidence, About this idea, About this notation).
- Collapsible sections the app keeps open across renders use a `<button aria-expanded>`; a disclosure that only reads uses `<details>`.
- Saving online goes only through `ProjectStore`; pages never call the API themselves, and anything about accounts shows only when `CloudAccount.IsAvailable`.
- Device preferences (`Projects/Preferences.cs`, `Settings/DeviceSettings.cs`) are never project data, and project data is never a device preference.
