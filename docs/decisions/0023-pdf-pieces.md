# 0023 · PDF pieces, read as they are

Status: accepted, 2026-11-05; amended 2026-11-05 (Andrei): recognising a PDF always makes a separate piece; that amendment reversed 2026-11-07 (testing item 16, Andrei, 2026-11-06: "the PDF is available right inside the piece; everything there is is picked up"): the notes go into the same piece, and a PDF can be kept inside a piece with notes. Andrei, 2026-11-04 ("new positioning: forScore + MuseScore with subtle AI"): a plain PDF is added to the library and shown as it is, with no interactivity, alongside a separate "Notes" view once it is recognised; each view keeps its own marks, and only a rough link from pages to bars is needed.

## Context

Until now a PDF could only be scanned (0011, 0014): the notes the scan server read became the piece, and the PDF itself was not kept. Players who read from printed scores want the page exactly as printed (fingerings, editor's marks, the engraver's layout), whether or not the notes are ever recognised, and they want it to cost nothing: reading a PDF on the device is free; recognition runs on the server and is Pro (0018).

## Decision

- **The PDF is kept with the piece, byte for byte.** `Project.Document` holds it and the project file writes it as `document` (base64, version 13, `docs/project-file.md`). A PDF piece has an empty `source`: `Project.HasNotes` is false and `OpenedProject.Open` refuses it, so nothing in the engine mistakes it for notes. `PdfDocument` (music-core) checks the `%PDF-` header and the size: up to 50 MB, since the file lives inside the project file in IndexedDB and passes through JS interop as one string.
- **It stays on the device.** A PDF piece is never queued for saving online: an online copy holds at most 2 MB. The PDF view says so in its overflow. Since [0026](0026-project-continuity.md) (section 2, C3) a plain PDF piece stays only in the iPad's score library and is not synced; a recognised PDF syncs as a project with notes while its PDF and marks stay on the iPad. Since version 0.5 (0026 C3, amended 2026-11-12) a plain PDF piece syncs too, without the PDF: its details, the PDF's description and its page marks.
- **The web app reads it with pdf.js**, vendored in `wwwroot/lib/pdfjs` (Apache-2.0; `tools/pdfjs/README.md` has the version, checksums and update steps) and loaded only when a PDF opens, like Verovio. The service worker caches it on first use, so a PDF opens offline after that. Only the Foxit standard fonts ship (their own licence); the Liberation fonts (GPL) and the QuickJS sandbox are left out, and pdf.js runs with `isEvalSupported: false`.
- **The PDF view is the score's paper, not a new screen.** `/pdf?project=…` shows the pages as sheets on the desk in one continuously scrolling document (0018 section 2), at each page's own proportions, one column in portrait and two to a row in landscape. Pages are drawn as they come near the view and let go when far from it. The bar is the score's: back to the library, the title, "p. 2 of 5", a PDF tag, and the overflow, with Download the PDF (the bytes as added). There is no transport, roll, Insights or selection: nothing on the page is music the app knows.
- **Where the player was is kept on the device.** The page under the bar is remembered per piece and reopened; Page 1 in the library opens at the top. A PDF piece has no resume points, Piece DNA or ideas until it has notes.
- **The library treats it as a piece.** Open a file accepts `.pdf` next to MIDI and MusicXML; the card shows "PDF · 3 pages" with page 1 as its cover, drawn by pdf.js.

- **Marks on the pages are the PDF view's own.** The pen writes on the pages with the score's pencil layer and palette (four inks, the highlighter, the eraser, undo, clear): each page is one frame its strokes are kept against, by its number, in units of a hundred and twentieth of the page's width, so a stroke keeps its place and size however wide the page is drawn. The bookmark in the bar marks the page under it (a carmine tab on its edge); the title opens the Contents, which lists the bookmarked pages to go to, name or remove. Both are kept in the project as `documentMarks` (`DocumentMarks` in music-core), apart from the notes' `marks`.

- **A metronome of its own.** The PDF has no notes to play, so the view has a metronome in a pill at the bottom, like the score's transport: start and stop, slower and faster by 4, the tempo tapped in time on its number, 2, 3, 4 or 6 beats to the bar (the first clicks higher) and a dot for each beat. It uses the playback engine's click and schedules it on the audio clock (`Scripts/metronome/metronome.ts`). It runs on the device and is free; the tempo and beats are kept per piece on the device.

- **Read the notes from the kept PDF, into the same piece** (since 2026-11-07). The PDF view's bar offers Recognise notes («Распознать ноты», the roll icon) while the PDF has pages and no notes (in the overflow until 2026-11-11, where Andrei did not find it; Pro on Motiff Lab's server, free on the player's own, as scanning is, 0011, 0014): the panel says where the PDF goes, with the same switch as Scan printed music, and sends the kept PDF as it is. The MusicXML the scan server returns becomes the piece's notes (`Project.Recognised`), its doubtful bars become Check flags, and the piece opens as notes with the Check drawer at the bar the page under the bar starts with. The PDF, its pages, marks and bookmarks stay exactly as they were, and the library shows one card for the piece. From 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-07 the notes became a separate piece that named the PDF piece (`readFrom`, `ReadFromPdf`); such pairs still open and switch between each other, and the library hides the PDF piece's card behind its notes.
- **Original PDF | Notes switches the two views of one piece.** Both views show the score's segmented control Original PDF | Notes («Оригинал PDF | Ноты», since 2026-11-11; PDF | Notes where the bar is short of room: on the web below 900 px across or beside a sheet below 1250 px, on the iPad wherever the whole bar does not fit): from the notes, the PDF opens at the page the first bar of the score page under the bar was read from (`DocumentPages`, a `PageMap`); from the PDF, the notes open at the bar that page starts with. With its notes read, the PDF's bar no longer offers Recognise notes, and Add a version opens the notes with Sources.
- **A PDF inside a piece with notes** (since 2026-11-07, 0022 section 1). Sources' Add a source keeps a PDF in a piece opened from a file or a recording (`Project.WithDocument`): its pages are shared evenly over the bars until its notes are read, PDF | Notes switches to it, and Read as a version sends it to the scan server and adds what it reads as another version from a score, linking its pages to the bars by the bar numbers the scan began each page with. The copy saved online leaves the PDF out (`Project.ForOnline`); it stays on the device.
- **Add a version on a PDF** (0022, section 1). The overflow also offers Add a version. Another version is compared with notes, so the panel first says the notes have to be read from the PDF and offers Read the notes, with Pro and the player's plan for a free player on Motiff Lab's server; the piece then opens as notes with Sources.
- **The link is rough.** The scan server marks the first measure of each page after the first with `<print new-page="yes"/>` (services/omr `merge.py`), music-core keeps where each page starts (`PageMap`) and shares the bars evenly when there are no marks.

## Later steps

1. The iPad app's PDF view (PDFKit) over the same project file. Since 2026-11-05 it shows the pages, reopens at the page the player was on, bookmarks pages and downloads the PDF (`PdfScreen`, over the facade's `document` calls, 0020), and the pen writes on the pages with the score's palette (PencilKit; each stroke drawn by PDFKit with its page, in the page's own spaces) and the metronome clicks in its pill (`Metronome`, its own audio engine with the playback engine's click); PDF | Notes switches between the two views of a piece with notes and a PDF kept inside it, the PDF view opened with `"view": "pdf"` and landing by `documentPages` (since 2026-11-07), or between a PDF piece and notes read into a piece of their own before, found by the notes' `readFrom` (`PdfNotesSwitch`, `OtherView`); Read the notes sends the kept PDF to the scan server set on the iPad, as on the web (`ScanServer`, `ReadNotesPanel`; the address in Settings, Account, when Motiff Lab's server is not offered), and the facade's `document.read` puts what it reads into the same piece, saved over its file and opened as notes at the page the player was on (since 2026-11-07). Sources on the iPad (`VersionsDrawer`) is the web app's sheet over the facade's `sources` calls and `performance.takeAll`.
